Cloverbank Farm

Grow your farm and get to know the neighbors across the river.

Quest journal

Talk to the people in Willowford to pick up quests.

    Walk with arrows or WASD. Face a tile and press Space or tap Use to interact. Fish from the east riverbank and reel in when a fish bites. Feed the hens by the southeast coop, then collect eggs the next morning. The east bridge leads to Willowford. Progress saves in this browser.
